    BLOCKMARSHAL

    Six generals. One minefield. Last crown standing wins.

    How to play
    • Your general (crown) and cities make +1 troop every turn. All your land makes +1 every 25 turns.
    • Drag a path from your tile, or tap your tile then a neighbour. Tap the selected tile again to move only half.
    • Mines hide under the ground. Tiles you hold show red dots on the side facing a neighbouring mine. A white dot points at a freshly planted mine: it can't be captured for 100 turns.
    • Stepping on a mine kills up to 15 troops, halves armies next to it and stuns you for 3 turns.
    • Long-press (or right-click) to flag a tile, up to 5. Only you see your flags.
    • Move onto a flagged mine to capture it instead of setting it off. Then long-press one of your own tiles (the same flag gesture) to plant it: enemies only see dots, you see the mine. Your own mines trip you too, unless you flag them. Long-press your own mine to flag/unflag it or pick it up and move it. A planted mine is locked for 100 turns (white ring): nobody can capture it and you can't pick it up.
    • Everyone sees every blast. The mark fades after 10 turns.
    • Long-press your general or a city you hold to move it to another of your open tiles on the same connected stretch of land. It can't touch another general or city, even at a corner, and can't move again for 100 turns.
    • Capture an enemy crown to take their empire. Last general standing wins.
    • 1 vs 1 is a duel on a smaller board, against another player (a bot steps in if nobody joins in time) or straight against a bot.
    • Tap ☰ for standings, Split, Undo, Clear, sound, feedback and leaving.
