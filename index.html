    BLOCKMARSHAL

    Conquer the map. Read the numbers. Don't step on a mine.

    Daily #1

    Custom game

    How to play
    • Your general (crown) and captured cities make +1 troop every turn. All your land makes +1 every 25 turns.
    • Tap your tile, then tap a neighbour to move all but one troop. Or drag a path from your tile. Tap the selected tile again to move only half.
    • Numbers work like minesweeper: how many of the 8 surrounding tiles hide a mine. Every tile you take reveals its number, and zeros open up the area.
    • Walking onto a mine kills up to 15 troops, halves every army next to it and cancels your queued moves.
    • Long-press (or right-click) to flag a suspected mine. Flags are just notes for you.
    • Capture an enemy general to take their whole empire. Last general standing wins.
