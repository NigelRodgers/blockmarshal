    BLOCKMARSHAL

    Six generals. One minefield. Last crown standing wins.

    How to play
    • Your general (crown) and cities make +1 troop every turn. All your land makes +1 every 25 turns.
    • Drag a path from your tile, or tap your tile then a neighbour. Tap the selected tile again to move only half.
    • Mines hide under the ground. Tiles you hold show red dots on the side facing a neighbouring mine.
    • Stepping on a mine kills up to 15 troops, halves armies next to it and stuns you for 3 turns.
    • Long-press (or right-click) to flag a tile. Only you can see your flags.
    • The eye is a watchtower: hold it to see further and spot mines around it.
    • Capture an enemy crown to take their empire. Last general standing wins.
